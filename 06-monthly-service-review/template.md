# Monthly service review pack: template

## How to use this template

**Purpose.** A standing monthly review of IT service performance, based on ITIL practices (incident, problem and change management, service level management and continual improvement). It answers four questions: how did we perform against what we promised, what is going wrong repeatedly, what is changing, and what are we doing about it.

**Guidance**

- Hold the review monthly, shortly after month end, with the service owners and a business representative. Keep it to 60 minutes.
- Circulate the pack two working days before. Use the meeting to discuss exceptions and agree actions, not to read the numbers aloud.
- Show a six-month trend for every measure so people see direction, not just a snapshot.
- Use the same definitions each month. Where a definition changes, restate history.
- Every Amber or Red measure needs a cause and an action. Actions go into the improvement register with an owner and a date, and are reviewed first at the next meeting.
- Separate incidents (restore service) from problems (find and remove the cause). Recurrence of the same incident type is the trigger to open a problem record.
- Include the user's voice: CSAT scores, response rate and two or three verbatim comments, good and bad.
