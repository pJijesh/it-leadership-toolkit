# Renewal checklist: managed service desk (fictional)

**Northwind Freight (fictional).** A worked example, shown part-way through the process on 4 November 2026. Suppliers, dates and figures are invented. It follows on from the [decision memo example](../07-technology-decision-memo/example-northwind.md).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Contract owner** | IT Manager |
| **Supplier** | Incumbent service desk provider (fictional) |
| **Service** | Managed service desk, 07:00 to 19:00 weekdays plus out-of-hours emergency line |
| **Annual cost (£)** | £108,000 |
| **Contract start / end date** | 1 April 2024 / 31 March 2027 |
| **Notice period and notice date** | 90 days. Notice must be received by 31 December 2026 |
| **Auto-renewal?** | Yes. Renews for 12 months at the then-current price plus indexation unless notice is given |
| **Approval level needed** | Board, because the three-year value exceeds the IT Manager's approval limit |

## 1. Confirm the facts

- [x] Read the contract and any amendments. Notice date confirmed as 31 December 2026.
- [x] Auto-renewal for 12 months confirmed. Price rises by the published indexation figure on renewal.
- [x] Termination rights, service credits (5 percent of the monthly fee per missed SLA month) and liability cap recorded.
- [x] Data return and exit assistance: ticket history is returned in a standard export within 30 days. Exit assistance is charged at a day rate.
- [x] Notice date in the shared calendar with reminders at 180, 120 and 60 days. The 60-day reminder falls on 1 November.
- [x] Contract register updated.

## 2. Review value and performance

- [x] Cost compared with budget: on budget at £9,000 a month.
- [x] SLA performance reviewed: resolution within SLA between 91 and 95 percent over six months against a 95 percent target. No service credits claimed so far because the breach threshold is set low.
- [x] Usage reviewed: ticket volumes steady at around 550 to 600 a month.
- [x] User feedback gathered: CSAT 4.2 to 4.4; main complaint is hand-offs.
- [x] Hard-to-replace elements listed: knowledge of the company's applications; out-of-hours cover.

## 3. Confirm the requirement

- [x] The need continues. It will grow with the number of sites.
- [x] Requirements for the next term written: integration with ticketing and identity platforms, out-of-hours cover, named service manager, security standards.
- [x] Checked against the IT strategy: no conflict. The strategy favours buying services over building them.

## 4. Test the market

- [x] Decision: competitive re-tender, because performance has been flat and contract value is significant.
- [x] Criteria and weights agreed before proposals were received. See the [vendor scorecard example](../04-vendor-scorecard/).
- [x] Cost and time to switch estimated: £8,000 to £12,000 one-off and about eight weeks.

## 5. Risk and compliance

- [ ] Security and data protection review of the preferred supplier. Due 4 December 2026.
- [ ] Supplier financial health check. Due 4 December 2026.
- [ ] Business continuity and exit plan confirmed. Due 18 December 2026.
- [x] Risk register updated: R-007 (contract ends before replacement is live).

## 6. Negotiate

- [ ] Agree price, three-year term with a 12-month break clause, fixed price for the initial term, service credits, indexation cap.
- [ ] Keep the incumbent as a credible fallback, with a written short extension option of up to three months.

## 7. Decide and approve

- [x] Decision memo written for the board.
- [ ] Board approval. Decision requested by 20 November 2026.
- [ ] Written notice to the incumbent served in the form the contract requires, with proof of delivery. Target 15 December 2026, which leaves two weeks of margin before 31 December.

## 8. Close out

- [ ] Sign and file the new contract. Raise the purchase order.
- [ ] Update contract register, calendar, budget and risk register.
- [ ] Schedule a performance review at month six of the new term.
- [ ] Record lessons: start the renewal process 12 months before the notice date for services of this size.
