# 30-60-90 day plan: IT Manager, Northwind Freight (fictional)

A worked example for a new IT Manager joining a fictional company. The people and details are invented.

**Start date:** Tuesday 5 January 2027 | **Manager:** Chief Operating Officer | **Review dates:** day 30 (4 February), day 60 (6 March), day 90 (5 April)

## What success looks like (agreed with manager)

| By day 30 | By day 60 | By day 90 |
|---|---|---|
| I have met every executive and every member of the IT team, and can describe the main risks and opportunities in plain English. | A prioritised plan exists and the first board IT report has gone out in an agreed format. | The strategy and roadmap are approved, two visible service improvements are live, and monthly service reviews are running. |

## Days 1 to 30: listen and learn

**Goal:** understand the business, the technology, the people and the risks.

| Area | Activities | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Stakeholders | Meet the five executives and each site manager in the first fortnight. Ask the same four questions: what works well, what frustrates you, what is coming that I should know about, how should I report to you. | Stakeholder map and list of themes |
| Team | One to one with each of the team members. Ask what they are proud of, what slows them down and what they would change first. | Team summary: skills, workload, concerns |
| Services | Read the last 12 months of tickets and major incidents. Sit with the service desk for two half-days. | Service baseline and top five irritants |
| Security and resilience | Check multi-factor authentication coverage, patch status, backup and last restore test. Read recent audit and penetration test reports. | Top five security and resilience risks |
| Suppliers and contracts | Build a contract register with owner, annual cost, end date and notice date for every IT supplier. | Contract register and the next three notice dates |
| Finance | Walk through budget, actuals and forecast with the Finance Director. Agree the monthly reporting routine. | Budget understanding; reconciliation routine |
| Architecture and assets | Review the system landscape, integrations and licence usage. List hardware older than its planned life. | System overview and asset list |

**Deliverable at day 30:** a two-page written summary: what I found, the top risks, three quick wins and what I need from the executive team. Shared with the Chief Operating Officer on 4 February.

## Days 31 to 60: plan and align

**Goal:** turn findings into a plan the business supports.

| Area | Activities | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Priorities | Rank issues by value and risk with the executive team. Agree the top three priorities. | Priority list signed off |
| Risk | Create the IT risk register from the findings. Agree owners. | Risk register with named owners |
| Measures | Choose around ten KPIs from the catalogue. Define each and record the baseline. | KPI baseline |
| Reporting | Draft the first one-page board IT report with the Finance Director. | First board report issued |
| Service | Run the first monthly service review. Fix the two most visible service irritants. | Review held; two fixes live |
| Suppliers | Create a renewal calendar and start the review of the next contract to expire. | Renewal calendar |
| Team | Agree one development goal for each person. Close the most critical skills gap. | Team plan |

**Deliverable at day 60:** draft IT strategy outline, a 12-month plan and a list of decisions needed from the board.

## Days 61 to 90: deliver and embed

**Goal:** show early results and set the rhythm for the year.

| Area | Activities | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Complete the agreed quick wins. Start the first priority project with a plan, budget and owner. | Quick wins delivered |
| Strategy | Present the strategy and three-year roadmap to the board. | Approved direction |
| Budget | Reforecast the year with finance and flag any risk to budget. | Reforecast agreed |
| Governance | Confirm who can approve what: change, spend and risk acceptance. | Decision rights written down |
| Communication | Agree a monthly update to all staff on what IT delivered and what is next. | Communications routine |

**Deliverable at day 90:** a review with the Chief Operating Officer against the success criteria above, and a plan for the next 90 days.

## Risks to the plan

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Peak trading period leaves little time for stakeholder meetings | Book meetings in the first two weeks; use short, structured conversations |
| An urgent incident or audit finding takes over the first month | Agree with the manager in week one what I will defer, and communicate it |
| A contract notice date falls inside the first 90 days | Build the contract register in the first fortnight and flag it immediately |

## Notes and learning log

Keep a dated log with three columns: what I learned, who told me, what I did about it. Review it weekly and share themes, not names, with the manager.
