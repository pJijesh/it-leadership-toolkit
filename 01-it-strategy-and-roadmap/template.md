# IT strategy and three-year roadmap: template

## How to use this template

**Purpose.** One document that explains what technology must do for the organisation over the next three years, what will be done first, what it will cost and how progress will be judged. It should be readable by a board member in ten minutes.

**Guidance**

- Start from the business strategy, not the technology. If the organisation has no written strategy, interview the executive team and write down what you heard, then ask them to confirm it.
- Keep the number of strategic themes to five or fewer. More than that is a list, not a strategy.
- Every initiative should trace to a theme, and every theme to a business outcome. Initiatives that trace to nothing are candidates to stop.
- Give cost as a range until a business case exists. State clearly what is committed and what is indicative.
- Say what you will not do. Boards trust a strategy that shows trade-offs.
- Review quarterly and re-issue yearly. A roadmap that never changes is not being used.
