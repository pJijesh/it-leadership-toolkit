# IT asset lifecycle plan: template

## How to use this template

**Purpose.** A plan for how hardware and software assets are acquired, run, refreshed and retired, so that replacement is planned and funded rather than forced by failure, and so that security, cost and environmental duties are met.

**Guidance**

- Start with an accurate inventory. You cannot plan the lifecycle of assets you cannot count. Record at least asset ID, type, model, owner or user, location, purchase date, warranty end, and support end date.
- Set a target life for each asset class, and a refresh trigger that is based on more than age: vendor support end, repair cost, performance and security.
- Convert the plan into a multi-year budget profile so that the cost is smoothed and visible. The target life divided into the fleet size gives the number to replace each year.
- Plan secure disposal before purchase. Wipe data to an agreed standard, keep the evidence, and use an authorised disposal or recycling provider that issues certificates.
- Review the plan every year and after any significant change in headcount, strategy or vendor support.
- Treat the target lives in the example as illustrations. Use the manufacturer's support dates and your own repair and failure history to set yours.
