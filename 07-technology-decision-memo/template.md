# Technology decision memo: template

## How to use this template

**Purpose.** A short, structured memo that lets a decision-maker choose between options knowing the cost, the risk and the recommendation. It is written to be read in five minutes and filed as the record of why a decision was made.

**Guidance**

- Put the decision and your recommendation in the first paragraph. Readers who stop there should still know what you are asking.
- Always include a "do nothing" or "continue as now" option. It is the baseline for the others.
- Show cost over a stated period (three or five years is usual), including one-off costs, running costs and the cost of change. State what is excluded.
- Give each option the same headings so they can be compared side by side.
- Be explicit about risk, assumptions and what you do not know. Do not hide uncertainty in the cost table.
- Say what would change your recommendation.
- Keep it to two pages. Move supporting evidence to appendices.
