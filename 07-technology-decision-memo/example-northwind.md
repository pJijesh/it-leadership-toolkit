# Decision memo: appointing a managed service desk provider

## 1. Decision and recommendation

The board is asked to approve Vendor B as the preferred supplier of the managed service desk from April 2027, subject to a satisfactory security review and contract terms. Vendor B scored highest on the weighted scorecard (4.30 out of 5, against 3.55 for the next best) and has the lowest three-year cost of the two leading options. The recommended contract is three years with a break clause at month 12. The three-year cost is £318,000 including transition, compared with £324,000 for extending the current contract on today's price.

## 2. Background

The current managed service desk contract costs £108,000 a year and expires on 31 March 2027. Notice of non-renewal must be given by 31 December 2026. Resolution within SLA has run at 91 to 95 percent against a 95 percent target over the last six months, and users report hand-offs between teams as their main frustration. A competitive process was run in October with four suppliers.

## 3. Requirements and constraints

- Must provide a 24-hour-a-day out-of-hours emergency line for priority 1 incidents.
- Must integrate with the company's ticketing and identity platforms.
- Must meet the company's security requirements for access to systems and data.
- Total cost must not exceed the current annual budget line of £108,000 for running cost.
- Transition must not take place in the peak trading weeks of November to early January.

## 4. Options

| | Option 1: Extend current contract | Option 2: Vendor B | Option 3: Vendor A |
|---|---|---|---|
| Description | Extend 12 months on current terms, then review | Appoint Vendor B for three years with a 12-month break clause | Appoint Vendor A for three years |
| Benefits | No transition risk; no change for users | Highest score; strongest integration; includes a named service manager | Familiar to the market; good security answers |
| Drawbacks | Performance below target; no improvement commitment; indexation risk | Transition effort; smaller team than Vendor A | Higher cost; integration needs extra work |
| Delivery effort and time | None | About eight weeks, planned for February to March 2027 | About ten weeks |
| Fit to requirements | Partly | Fully, subject to security review | Mostly |

Vendors C and D were also assessed. Vendor C offered the lowest price but scored lower on integration and service commitments. Vendor D scored lower on cost and transition risk. Neither was shortlisted.

## 7. Evaluation

Eight criteria were weighted before proposals were opened (service quality 25 percent, cost 20, security 15, integration 10, transition risk 10, references 10, commercial flexibility 5, account management 5). Four panel members scored independently and then agreed moderated scores. Results: Vendor B 4.30, Vendor A 3.55, Vendor C 3.50, Vendor D 3.45. Moving any single weight up or down by five points (with the total rebased to 100) does not change which vendor ranks first. Reference calls and the security review are still to be completed for Vendor B.

## 8. Recommendation

Appoint Vendor B, with these conditions:

1. A satisfactory security review and data processing agreement.
2. Two positive reference calls from customers of similar size.
3. A 12-month break clause, a fixed price for the initial term and service credits for missed SLAs.
4. Transition scheduled for February and March 2027, with an agreed fallback of a short extension of the current contract (up to three months).

This recommendation would change if the security review finds a material gap, or if the reference calls contradict the evaluation.
